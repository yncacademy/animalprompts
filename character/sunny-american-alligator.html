Cute American alligator named SUNNY, with oversized turquoise eyes, rounded dark-olive scales, a cream jaw, a broad friendly snout, short legs, a strong tail, and a tiny yellow sun hat. A relaxed wetland wanderer

high-end 3D animated feature reference sheet, portrait 4:5 board, original character, header “SUNNY” with tagline “COOL WATER / WARM HEART,” traits “CALM / FRIENDLY / PATIENT / CURIOUS”

full-body turnaround — front / 3-4 / side / back / T-pose, jaw shape, scale ridges, tail, hat, feet, and proportions consistent, labels beneath each pose

row of 4 expressions: gentle toothy smile, curious eye lift, surprised splash face, sleepy basking expression

palette swatches DARK OLIVE SCALES, CREAM JAW, TURQUOISE EYES, MOSS GREEN, SUN YELLOW

Florida wetland habitat accents with cypress knees, reeds, lily pads, shallow water, and warm haze in the information band, approximate length “APPROX. 70 CM,” bio about Sunny spending peaceful afternoons basking and waving hello to passing dragonflies

bottom macro panels labeled EYE DETAIL, SCALE TEXTURE, JAW DETAIL, SUN HAT
