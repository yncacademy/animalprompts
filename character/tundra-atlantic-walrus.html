Cute Atlantic walrus named TUNDRA, with oversized sea-green eyes, warm brown skin, a round whiskered muzzle, two small ivory tusks, flipper-like limbs, and a knitted blue neck warmer. A calm Arctic beach guardian

high-end 3D animated feature reference sheet, portrait 4:5 board, original character, header “TUNDRA” with tagline “SOFT WHISKERS / STRONG SPIRIT,” traits “CALM / FRIENDLY / BRAVE / PROTECTIVE”

full-body turnaround — front / 3-4 / side / back / T-pose, tusks, whiskers, neck warmer, flippers, and body proportions consistent, labels beneath each pose

row of 4 expressions: proud tusk smile, curious whisker twitch, surprised splash face, sleepy ice-floe grin
