Reference sheet prompt

Cute clownfish named NEMOA, with oversized violet eyes, bright tangerine scales, three creamy white stripes, tiny rounded fins, and a small sea-anemone petal clip. A cheerful reef neighbor

high-end 3D animated feature reference sheet, portrait 4:5 board, original character, header “NEMOA” with tagline “BRIGHT STRIPES / BRIGHTER SPIRIT,” traits “BOLD / FRIENDLY / CURIOUS / PLAYFUL”

full-body turnaround — front / 3-4 / side / back / T-pose, stripe placement, fins, clip, tail, and proportions consistent, labels beneath each pose

row of 4 expressions: joyful open-mouth grin, curious fin raise, startled bubble face, sleepy anemone cuddle expression

palette swatches TANGERINE SCALES, CREAM STRIPES, BLACK FIN EDGES, VIOLET EYES, ANEMONE PINK

tropical coral reef habitat accents with anemone fronds, coral shapes, bubbles, and turquoise water in the information band, approximate length “APPROX. 18 CM,” bio about NEMOA welcoming everyone to the reef with bright colors and an even brighter smile
