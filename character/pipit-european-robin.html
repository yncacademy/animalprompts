Reference sheet prompt

Cute European robin named PIPIT, with oversized dark eyes, soft brown back feathers, a vivid orange breast, tiny black beak, delicate legs, and a small red berry scarf. A brave little winter singer

high-end 3D animated feature reference sheet, portrait 4:5 board, original character, header “PIPIT” with tagline “TINY BIRD / BIG SONG,” traits “BRAVE / MUSICAL / FRIENDLY / CURIOUS”

full-body turnaround — front / 3-4 / side / back / T-pose, orange breast patch, scarf, beak, legs, and proportions consistent, labels beneath each pose

row of 4 expressions: joyful singing beak-open pose, curious head tilt, proud chest puff, sleepy feather tuck

palette swatches WARM BROWN FEATHERS, ORANGE BREAST, CREAM BELLY, DARK EYES, BERRY RED

European woodland and garden habitat accents with holly leaves, bare branches, berries, and soft winter haze in the information band, approximate height “APPROX. 14 CM,” bio about Pipit singing bravely through every season and welcoming lonely winter mornings

bottom macro panels labeled EYE DETAIL, BREAST FEATHERS, BEAK DETAIL, BERRY SCARF

8k render, soft feather detail, no extra robins, no logos
