Reference sheet prompt

Cute spotted pufferfish named PUFF, with oversized amber eyes, a round pale-yellow body, mint-green spots, tiny fins, a small beak-like mouth, and a blue bubble charm. A cautious little reef comedian

high-end 3D animated feature reference sheet, portrait 4:5 board, original character, header “PUFF” with tagline “SMALL FINS / BIG SURPRISES,” traits “CHEERFUL / CAUTIOUS / FUNNY / CURIOUS”

full-body turnaround — exactly five views: front / 3-4 / side / back / T-pose, spot pattern, fins, mouth, charm, and body proportions consistent, labels beneath each pose

row of 4 expressions: happy round smile, suspicious squint, dramatically puffed-up shock, sleepy floating face

palette swatches PALE YELLOW, MINT SPOTS, SOFT WHITE BELLY, AMBER EYES, BUBBLE BLUE

shallow coral reef habitat accents with rocks, sea plants, bubbles, and warm turquoise water in the information band, approximate length “APPROX. 20 CM,” bio about Puff proving that a tiny fish can have the biggest reaction in the reef

bottom macro panels labeled EYE DETAIL, SPOTTED SKIN, TINY FIN, BUBBLE CHARM

8k render, smooth slightly glossy skin, no extra fish, no logos
