Cute giant anteater named NOODLE, with oversized dark eyes, a long tapered snout, shaggy brown-and-cream fur, a dramatic diagonal shoulder stripe, enormous fluffy tail, and a tiny red bow tie. A gentle insect hunter with a wonderfully unusual silhouette

high-end 3D animated feature reference sheet, portrait 4:5 board, original character, header “NOODLE” with tagline “LONG SNOUT / BIG HEART,” traits “GENTLE / QUIET / CURIOUS / DETERMINED”

full-body turnaround — exactly five views: front / 3-4 / side / back / T-pose, long snout, stripe, tail, bow tie, claws, and proportions identical, labels beneath each pose

row of 4 expressions: curious tongue peek, delighted ant discovery, surprised raised-snout look, sleepy tail-wrapped smile
