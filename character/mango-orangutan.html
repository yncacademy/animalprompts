Cute orangutan named MANGO, with oversized copper eyes, shaggy orange fur, rounded cheeks, long arms, small ears, a cream muzzle, and a leaf-shaped sun hat. A thoughtful canopy artist

high-end 3D animated feature reference sheet, portrait 4:5 board, original character, header “MANGO” with tagline “ORANGE FUR / GOLDEN IDEAS,” traits “THOUGHTFUL / CREATIVE / GENTLE / CURIOUS”

full-body turnaround — front / 3-4 / side / back / T-pose, long-arm proportions, hat, muzzle, fur, and hands consistent, labels beneath each pose

row of 4 expressions: warm smile, curious brow lift, delighted fruit grin, sleepy branch-hugging face

palette swatches ORANGE FUR, CREAM MUZZLE, COPPER EYES, LEAF GREEN, GOLDEN TAN

Southeast Asian rainforest canopy habitat accents with hanging vines, large leaves, mist, and tropical fruit shapes in the information band, approximate height “APPROX. 80 CM,” bio about Mango finding beauty in every branch and turning leaves into little works of art

bottom macro panels labeled EYE DETAIL, SHAGGY FUR, HAND DETAIL, LEAF HAT

8k render, rich fur texture, long expressive arms, no extra orangutans, no logos
