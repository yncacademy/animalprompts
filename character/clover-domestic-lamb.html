Cute domestic lamb named CLOVER, with oversized sky-blue eyes, fluffy white wool, a soft peach muzzle, tiny rounded ears, little charcoal hooves, and a lavender flower crown. A gentle pasture dreamer

high-end 3D animated feature reference sheet, portrait 4:5 board, original character, header “CLOVER” with tagline “SOFT WOOL / SOFTER HEART,” traits “GENTLE / SHY / CURIOUS / KIND”

full-body turnaround — front / 3-4 / side / back / T-pose, wool silhouette, flower crown, ears, hooves, and face consistent, labels beneath each pose

row of 4 expressions: bashful smile, curious ear lift, joyful open-mouth bleat, sleepy woolly yawn

palette swatches CLOUD-WHITE WOOL, PEACH MUZZLE, CHARCOAL HOOVES, LAVENDER FLOWERS, SAGE STEMS
