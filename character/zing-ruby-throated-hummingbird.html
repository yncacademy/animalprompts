Cute ruby-throated hummingbird named ZING, with oversized black eyes, iridescent emerald feathers, a vivid ruby throat, a tiny pointed beak, rapid delicate wings, and a miniature flower pendant. A joyful garden spark

high-end 3D animated feature reference sheet, portrait 4:5 board, original character, header “ZING” with tagline “TINY WINGS / HUGE ENERGY,” traits “FAST / CURIOUS / FEARLESS / CHEERFUL”

full-body turnaround — front / 3-4 / side / back / T-pose, wing transparency, ruby throat, beak, tail feathers, pendant, and proportions consistent, labels beneath each pose

row of 4 expressions: joyful open-beak chirp, curious eye sparkle, startled wing flare, sleepy flower-hover expression

palette swatches EMERALD FEATHERS, RUBY THROAT, CHARCOAL EYES, IVORY BEAK, FLOWER PINK

flower-garden habitat accents with tubular blossoms, soft leaves, pollen specks, and warm morning light in the information band, approximate length “APPROX. 10 CM,” bio about Zing making every garden feel like a fast-moving festival of color

bottom macro panels labeled EYE DETAIL, IRIDESCENT FEATHERS, WING DETAIL, FLOWER PENDANT
